Why it matters

A 25-year asset deserves 25 years of care

Solar and battery systems are built to last decades — but only if someone is watching. Left alone, small faults quietly cost you generation, and warranties rarely cover the callout to put them right.

Faults hide in plain sight

A dropped string or failing optimiser can quietly cost you generation for months before you would ever notice.

Warranties have limits

Manufacturer warranties cover the hardware — but often not the labour, callout or diagnosis to put things right.

Performance drifts

Soiling, shading and connection wear all chip away at output if nothing is monitoring it.

Life admin piles up

Chasing manufacturers, booking engineers and tracking claims is time you would rather not spend.
