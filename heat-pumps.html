Yes. Modern air source heat pumps are designed for the UK climate and keep working efficiently well below freezing. The key is a properly designed system — which is exactly what our survey delivers, sizing everything to your home so it's warm all winter.
Because a heat pump is 3–4 times more efficient than a boiler, running costs are similar to or lower than gas on the right electricity tariff today — and far cheaper when paired with solar. You also dramatically cut your carbon and future-proof against gas price rises.
