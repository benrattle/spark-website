Our commitment

We aim to get everything right first time, but if something falls short we want to know so we can put it right. We treat every complaint seriously, fairly and in confidence, and we use what we learn to improve.

Step 1 — Get in touch

The quickest way to resolve an issue is to contact our customer care team directly by phone or email. Please give us your name, address, installation reference if you have one, and a description of the problem. Most concerns are resolved at this first stage.

Step 2 — Formal complaint

If you would like to make a formal complaint, put it in writing to care@sparkhome.co.uk. We will acknowledge it within three working days and aim to provide a full response within ten working days. If we need longer to investigate, we will keep you updated.

Step 3 — Review

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can ask for your complaint to be reviewed by a senior member of our team, who will take a fresh look and respond with our final position.

Step 4 — Independent escalation

As an accredited installer, our work is covered by independent consumer protection schemes. If we cannot resolve your complaint, you may refer it to the relevant body — such as HIES or the RECC alternative dispute resolution service — for an impartial review.

Finance complaints

If your complaint relates to a finance agreement and we are unable to resolve it, you may be able to refer it to the Financial Ombudsman Service. We will provide the relevant details with our final response.

How to reach us

Phone: call our customer care team during opening hours. Email: care@sparkhome.co.uk. Post: write to us at our registered office address. Please include your installation reference where possible so we can help you faster.

Need to raise a complaint? Contact our customer care team at care@sparkhome.co.uk or on 0330 818 7273.
