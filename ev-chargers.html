Buyer's guide

How to choose a home EV charger

Four things that decide which charger is right for your home and your car.

01

Tethered or untethered?

Tethered has a fixed cable — plug straight in, no fuss. Untethered uses your own cable for a tidier look and future-proofing. Most drivers prefer tethered for convenience.

02

7kW or 22kW?

Almost all homes use 7kW, which fully charges most EVs overnight. 22kW needs a three-phase supply that few homes have. We check your supply before recommending.

03

Smart features

A smart charger schedules charging for the cheapest off-peak hours and lets you control everything from an app — the single biggest way to cut your charging costs.

04

Solar-ready

If you have (or might add) solar, a solar-compatible charger diverts free daytime energy into your car. It's the cheapest possible way to drive — see the savings below.

The natural next step

Why EV owners go solar next

Faster solar payback

An EV roughly doubles your home's electricity use. The more you use, the more a solar system saves — so panels pay for themselves sooner.

Charge on free sunshine

Solar generates most during the day. A battery stores it so you can top up the car on your own energy — even after dark — instead of buying from the grid.

Lock in your rate

As electricity prices rise, charging from your own roof protects you. Your cost per mile stays low for decades, whatever happens to energy prices.
