Please note: This is a template for guidance and must be reviewed and approved by a qualified solicitor before publication. It is not legal advice.

1. Who we are

This policy explains how Spark (a trading name of Spark Energy UK Ltd) collects, uses and protects your personal data. We are the data controller for the information you provide to us, and we handle it in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly — such as your name, contact details, postcode, property details and energy usage — when you request a quote, register interest or contact us. We also collect limited technical data, such as your device and browsing activity, when you use our website.

3. How we use your information

We use your data to prepare and provide quotations, design and deliver your installation, arrange finance where requested, provide aftercare and support, and — where you have agreed — to keep you informed about relevant products and offers.

4. Our lawful basis

We process your data on the basis of your consent, to take steps at your request before entering into a contract, to perform a contract with you, to comply with legal obligations, and for our legitimate interests in running and improving our business.

5. Sharing your information

We share data only where necessary — for example with our installation engineers, finance partners such as Hometree, accreditation bodies, and trusted service providers who act on our behalf. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes we collected it for, including to satisfy legal, accounting, warranty or reporting requirements.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to our use of your data, and to data portability. You can withdraw consent for marketing at any time. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to make it work, to remember your preferences and to understand how it is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings. A full cookie notice is available on request.

9. Contact & complaints

If you have any concern about how we handle your data, please contact us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK supervisory authority for data protection.
