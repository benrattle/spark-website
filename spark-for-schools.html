Every Spark install helps power a school

For every home we fit with solar, we donate a solar panel. They build up — and once we have enough, we give a local school a complete solar system of its own.

How it works

Three simple steps

01

You go solar with Spark

Choose Spark for your home solar and battery system, just as you normally would.

02

We donate a panel

For every single installation we complete, we set aside one solar panel for the Spark for Schools fund.

03

We power a school

Once enough panels build up, we donate and install a complete solar system for a school — cutting its energy bills for decades.

Why take part

Good for your home, local schools and the planet

Going solar with Spark already cuts your bills. Now it does something more — every install moves a local school closer to its own free solar power, at no cost to the school.

✓

Free for schools

Schools pay nothing — the system is fully donated and installed.

✓

Lasting impact

A complete solar system, not a one-off cheque.

✓

Lower school bills

Freeing up budget to spend on teaching and pupils.

✓

Greener future

Helping the next generation learn about clean energy.
