HOME BATTERY STORAGE

Store your solar. Power your evenings.

Panels generate most during the day — but you use most energy at night. A home battery banks your free daytime solar so you can run your home on it after dark, and keeps the lights on if the grid goes down.

Why add a battery

What storage does for you

Use more of your own solar

Store daytime generation instead of exporting it cheaply, and use it when you actually need it.

Dodge the evening peak

Run your home on stored energy through the most expensive hours of the day.

Charge off-peak

Top up from cheap overnight tariffs and use that energy when rates are high.

Backup in an outage

Keep essential circuits powered automatically if the grid goes down.

How it works

From rooftop to evening in three steps

  1. Charge by day. Your panels power the home and any surplus charges the battery instead of being exported.
  2. Use by night. As the sun drops, your home draws from the battery rather than buying expensive grid power.
  3. Top up smart. If needed, the battery tops up from cheap off-peak electricity, ready for the next peak.
